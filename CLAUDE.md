## Design Context

### Users

Anser serves self-hosted operators who configure routes and investigate delivery failures, and people who send and receive notifications through the NSH client. Favor clear status, quick repeat actions, and recovery in the context where an error occurred.

### Brand Personality

Calm, warm, and precise. Use 雁字 in Chinese and Anser in English. Keep the supplied logo and the copper Kubai navigation icons.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use Elastic UI for the embedded management console and NSH native controls for the standalone client. Support system, light, and dark appearance. Use typography and spacing to group related work. Reserve strong shadows for overlays, and leave native window chrome to the platform.

### Design Principles

- Put the current task and its primary action on the same visual axis.
- Keep desktop keyboard navigation immediate and provide comfortable touch targets on mobile.
- Keep pending, success, and failure feedback beside the action it describes. Preserve input when submissions fail.
- Use short motion for feedback and spatial changes, and respect reduced motion and transparency preferences.
- Check narrow layouts and both languages with realistic content, including long account names and delivery errors.
